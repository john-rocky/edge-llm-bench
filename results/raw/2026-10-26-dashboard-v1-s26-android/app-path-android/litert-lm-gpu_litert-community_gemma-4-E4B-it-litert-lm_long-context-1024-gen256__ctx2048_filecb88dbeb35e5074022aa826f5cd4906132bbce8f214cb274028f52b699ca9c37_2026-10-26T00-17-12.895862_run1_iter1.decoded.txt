Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, zero-latency voice transcription:** On-device models can transcribe speech instantly as it is spoken, without the delay of sending audio to a server and waiting for a response. This is crucial for live captioning during a phone call or meeting where even a slight delay is disruptive.
2. **Instantaneous facial recognition for security:** Biometric authentication, such as unlocking a phone using a face scan, must happen locally for privacy and speed. Sending a live video feed to the cloud for verification would introduce unacceptable latency and massive privacy risks.
3. **Offline language translation:** A phone can translate text or speech into another language even when there is no internet connection. This allows users to communicate in remote areas or during travel where cellular service is unavailable.
4. **Immediate photo enhancement and editing:** AI-powered features like portrait mode depth mapping or real-time object removal can process images instantly on the device's GPU. Cloud processing would require uploading the high-resolution image, processing it, and then re-downloading the edited version.
