Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, zero-latency voice commands:** On-device AI can process voice input and execute commands instantly without waiting for a network round trip. This allows for immediate interaction, such as turning off a light or setting a timer, even when the phone has no internet connection.
2. **Instantaneous local photo tagging and search:** AI models running locally can analyze images immediately after they are taken to identify objects, people, or scenes. This enables users to search their photo library for "dogs in the park" without uploading every picture to a remote server.
3. **Privacy-preserving biometric authentication:** Facial recognition and fingerprint analysis can be performed entirely on the device's secure enclave. This ensures that sensitive biometric data never leaves the phone, providing a higher level of security than cloud-based verification.
4. **Offline language translation for short phrases:** Small, optimized translation models can run locally to translate quick conversations or signs without needing a data connection. This is crucial for travelers in areas with poor or no cellular service.
