Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, low-latency voice commands:** On-device AI can process spoken commands instantly without needing to transmit audio data to a server. This is crucial for immediate interactions like controlling a smart home device or initiating a quick search.
2. **Privacy-sensitive personal data processing:** Since the data stays on the device, sensitive information like private messages or location history is not exposed to external servers. This offers a significant security advantage over cloud models where data must be uploaded for processing.
3. **Offline functionality for core tasks:** Many on-device models allow users to perform essential tasks, such as basic translation or text summarization, even when there is no internet connection. This ensures functionality in areas with poor or no connectivity.
4. **Instantaneous on-device image recognition:** A phone can instantly identify objects, faces, or text within a photo without needing to upload the image to a cloud service. This is vital for quick actions like tagging a picture or identifying a plant in the field.
