input_prompt: Explain what on-device AI means in simple terms.
INFO: Loaded OpenCL library with dlopen.
Imagine you have a smartphone. Normally, when you ask it to do something smart—like recognize a face, translate a sentence, or suggest the next word you want to type—the phone has to send that information to a giant computer far away (the "cloud") to get the answer back.

**On-device AI** is when the "brain" for that smart task is built **directly inside your phone or device**, instead of needing to send it to a faraway computer.

Here is a simple breakdown using analogies:

---

### 🧠 The "Cloud" Way (The Old Way)

Think of the cloud like a **restaurant kitchen**.

* **You (the phone):** You have a request (e.g., "What is this picture of?").
* **The Kitchen (the Cloud Server):** You have to call the kitchen, send them the picture, and wait for them to cook it and send the answer back.
* **The Problem:** This takes time (latency), and it uses up your internet data.

### 📱 The "On-Device" Way (The New Way)

Think of on-device AI like having a **mini-chef in your own kitchen**.

* **You (the phone):** You have a request.
* **The Mini-Chef (the AI chip inside your phone):** The chef is already standing right there. They look at the picture and give you the answer instantly.
* **The Benefit:** It’s super fast, it doesn't need the internet to work, and your private data never has to leave your phone.

---

### 💡 Why is this a big deal? (The Benefits)

1. **Speed (Instant Results):** Because the processing happens right there, things happen instantly. No waiting for a signal to travel across the world.
2. **Privacy (Your Data Stays Home):** This is the biggest win. If your phone is recognizing your face or listening to your voice, that sensitive information never has to be sent to a company's server. It stays on your device.
3. **Works Anywhere (No Internet Needed):** You can use smart features even when you are in a basement, on a plane, or in a remote area with no Wi-Fi or cell service.

### 🛠️ Where do you see it?

* **Face ID:** Your phone recognizes your face without sending a picture to the internet.
* **Real-time Translation:** Translating a conversation as it happens, without a delay.
* **Smart Camera Features:** Automatically blurring the background (Portrait Mode) or identifying objects in a photo instantly.
* **Siri/Google Assistant:** Basic commands being processed directly on the phone.

**In short: On-device AI means making your device smarter by putting the "brain" inside it, making it faster, more private, and more reliable.**
