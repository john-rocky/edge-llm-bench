Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, low-latency voice commands:** On-device models can process spoken commands instantly without needing to transmit audio data to a server. This is crucial for features like live transcription or immediate device control.
2. **Instantaneous local image recognition:** A phone can identify objects, faces, or scenes directly from a photo stored on the device. This allows for immediate actions like tagging a picture or applying a filter without a network delay.
3. **Privacy-sensitive data processing:** Sensitive personal data, such as location history or private messages, can be analyzed entirely on the device. This minimizes the risk of data exposure during transmission to a remote server.
4. **Offline functionality for core tasks:** Essential AI features, like basic translation or text summarization, can operate even when the internet connection is completely unavailable. This ensures basic functionality remains accessible regardless of connectivity.
