Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, zero-latency voice transcription:** On-device models can transcribe speech instantly as it is spoken, without the delay of sending audio to a server and waiting for a response. This is crucial for live captioning during a meeting or conversation where even a slight delay is disruptive.
2. **Instantaneous local image recognition:** A phone can identify objects, faces, or scenes in a photo immediately after it is taken, without needing an internet connection. This allows for features like instant translation of signs or identifying plants while offline.
3. **Privacy-preserving biometric authentication:** On-device AI can process facial recognition or fingerprint data locally to unlock the phone. This ensures that sensitive biometric templates never leave the device, maintaining a high level of user privacy.
4. **Offline language translation:** Certain smaller, optimized language models can run locally to translate text or speech when there is no network access. This allows travelers to communicate in foreign areas where cellular service is unavailable.
5. **Immediate gesture recognition for control:** On-device AI can interpret complex hand or body gestures captured by the camera or sensors to control the phone's functions
