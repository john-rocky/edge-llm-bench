Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, zero-latency voice transcription:** On-device models can transcribe speech instantly as it is spoken, without the delay of sending audio to a server and waiting for a response. This is crucial for live captioning during a meeting or conversation where even a slight delay is disruptive.
2. **Instantaneous facial recognition for security:** Local AI can analyze a face in real-time to verify identity for unlocking a phone or authorizing a payment. This process happens entirely on the device, ensuring that biometric data never leaves the phone's secure enclave.
3. **Immediate photo object detection for accessibility:** A phone can instantly identify objects, people, or scenes within a camera feed to describe them to a visually impaired user. This happens at the speed of the camera shutter, providing immediate context without network dependency.
4. **Offline language translation for basic phrases:** Small, optimized models can translate short, common phrases between languages without an internet connection. This allows travelers to communicate basic needs in remote areas where cellular service is unavailable.
5. **Local noise cancellation during calls:** On-device signal processing can filter out background noise from the microphone
