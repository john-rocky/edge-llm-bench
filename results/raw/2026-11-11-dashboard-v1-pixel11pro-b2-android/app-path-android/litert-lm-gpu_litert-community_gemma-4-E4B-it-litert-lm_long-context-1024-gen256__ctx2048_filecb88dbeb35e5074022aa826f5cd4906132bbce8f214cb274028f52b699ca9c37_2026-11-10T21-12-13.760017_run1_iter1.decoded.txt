Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, zero-latency voice transcription:** On-device models can transcribe speech instantly without sending audio to a server, which is crucial for live captioning or hands-free dictation where even a slight delay is disruptive. This ensures the user experience remains seamless and immediate, regardless of network connectivity.
2. **Instantaneous local image recognition:** A phone can analyze a photo for objects, text, or scenes immediately after the shutter clicks, allowing for instant tagging or search results. This is vital for quick identification tasks, such as recognizing a plant or a landmark while you are still looking at it.
3. **Privacy-preserving biometric authentication:** On-device AI processes facial recognition or fingerprint data locally on the secure enclave of the phone. This means sensitive biometric templates never leave the device, offering a higher level of privacy than sending data to a remote cloud server.
4. **Offline language translation:** Certain smaller, optimized language models can run entirely on the device to translate short phrases or text snippets when there is no internet access. This allows travelers or users in remote areas to communicate without relying on a data connection.
