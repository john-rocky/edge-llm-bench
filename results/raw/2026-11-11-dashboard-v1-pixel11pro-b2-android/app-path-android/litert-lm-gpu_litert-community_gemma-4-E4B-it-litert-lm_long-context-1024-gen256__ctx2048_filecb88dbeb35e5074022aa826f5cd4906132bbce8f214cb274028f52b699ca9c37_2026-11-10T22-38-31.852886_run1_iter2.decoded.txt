Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, zero-latency voice transcription:** On-device models can transcribe speech instantly as it is spoken, without the delay of sending audio to a server and waiting for a response. This is crucial for live captioning during a meeting or a phone call where even a slight delay is disruptive.
2. **Instantaneous local image recognition:** A phone can identify objects, faces, or scenes in a photo immediately after it is taken, without needing an internet connection. This allows for features like instant translation of signs or identifying plants while you are offline.
3. **Privacy-preserving biometric authentication:** Face ID and fingerprint recognition run entirely on the device's secure enclave, meaning sensitive biometric data never leaves the phone. This ensures that your most personal security credentials are never exposed to a third-party cloud service.
4. **Offline language translation:** Certain basic or frequently used phrases can be translated locally using a small, optimized model. This allows travelers to communicate in a foreign language even when they have no cellular or Wi-Fi signal.
