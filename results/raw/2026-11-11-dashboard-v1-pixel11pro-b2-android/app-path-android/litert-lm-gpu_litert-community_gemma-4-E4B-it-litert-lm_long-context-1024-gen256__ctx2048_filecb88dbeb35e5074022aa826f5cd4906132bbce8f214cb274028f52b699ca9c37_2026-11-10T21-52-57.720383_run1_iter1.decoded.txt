Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, zero-latency voice commands:** On-device AI can process voice commands instantly without needing to send audio data to a remote server. This allows for immediate responses, such as turning off a flashlight or setting a timer, which is critical for safety and usability.
2. **Instantaneous local image recognition:** A phone can analyze a photo for objects or text immediately after it is taken, without any network delay. This enables features like instant translation of signs or identifying plants while the user is still looking at the image.
3. **Privacy-preserving biometric authentication:** Face ID or fingerprint recognition runs entirely on the device's secure enclave. This ensures that sensitive biometric data never leaves the phone, providing a high level of security that cloud-based systems cannot match.
4. **Offline language translation:** Certain basic or pre-downloaded language packs can be processed locally by the phone's AI. This allows users to translate text or speech in areas with no internet connectivity, such as remote travel destinations.
