1. **Real-time Neural Translation**
   A cloud model cannot translate text instantly because it requires a server to process the data and store it in memory, which introduces latency and delays. On the other hand, a phone's AI processor runs directly on the device's hardware, allowing for near-instantaneous translation without waiting for a server response.

2. **Automatic Video Transcription**
   Cloud models are limited by their ability to process video streams in real-time, often requiring the user to pause or wait for the video to load before transcription can begin. A phone's AI can capture the video stream and transcribe it immediately as the user speaks, providing immediate feedback without any waiting period.

3. **Context-Aware Image Captioning**
   Cloud models struggle with context because they cannot understand the specific scene or the user's intent when generating a caption, often resulting in generic or irrelevant descriptions. A phone's AI can analyze the visual content and the user's context to generate a highly relevant and accurate caption that fits the specific situation.
