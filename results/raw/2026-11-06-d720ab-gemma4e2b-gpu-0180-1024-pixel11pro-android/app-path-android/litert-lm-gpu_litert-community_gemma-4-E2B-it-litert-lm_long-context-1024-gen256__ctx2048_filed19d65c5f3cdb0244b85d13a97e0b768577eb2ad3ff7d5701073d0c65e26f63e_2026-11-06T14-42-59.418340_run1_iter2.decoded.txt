Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, low-latency voice commands:** On-device AI can process spoken commands instantly without needing to transmit audio data to a server. This is crucial for features like live transcription or immediate device control.
2. **Instantaneous biometric security checks:** AI models running locally can analyze facial expressions or fingerprint scans immediately to unlock or verify access to the device. This ensures privacy and extremely fast authentication without network delay.
3. **Offline language translation:** The model can perform translation tasks entirely on the phone, even in areas with no internet connection. This capability is vital for users in remote locations or during network outages.
4. **Privacy-sensitive personal data processing:** Since the data never leaves the device, on-device AI offers a high degree of privacy for sensitive information like health metrics or private messages. This eliminates the risk associated with sending personal data to third-party servers.
5. **Ultra-fast image and video filtering:** Applying complex visual effects or real-time object recognition to a camera feed happens locally, resulting in near-instantaneous feedback. This is necessary for live gaming overlays or immediate photo enhancements.
