Imagine you have a smart assistant on your phone, like Siri or Google Assistant.

**In the old way (Cloud AI):** When you ask a question, your phone has to send that question *over the internet* to a giant computer somewhere else (the "cloud"). That big computer does the thinking, figures out the answer, and then sends the answer *back* to your phone.

**On-Device AI is different. It means the "thinking" happens right there, on your own device (your phone, your laptop, etc.).**
