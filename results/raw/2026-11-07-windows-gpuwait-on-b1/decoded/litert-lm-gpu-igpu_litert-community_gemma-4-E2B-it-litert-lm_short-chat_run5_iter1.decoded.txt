Imagine a computer or a device (like your smartphone, smart watch, or a camera) that has its own little bit of **smart thinking power** built right into it, instead of needing to send everything to a giant, faraway server (like the internet) to do the thinking.

**On-device AI simply means that the AI "lives" and runs directly on the device you are using.**
