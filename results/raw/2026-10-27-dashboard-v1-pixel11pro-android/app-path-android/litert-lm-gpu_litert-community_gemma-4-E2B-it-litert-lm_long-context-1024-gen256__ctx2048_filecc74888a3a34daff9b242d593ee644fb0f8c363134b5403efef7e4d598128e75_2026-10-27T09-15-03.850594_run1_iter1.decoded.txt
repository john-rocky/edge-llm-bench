Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, low-latency voice commands:** On-device AI can process spoken commands instantly without needing to transmit audio data to a server first. This is crucial for features like live transcription or immediate device control.
2. **Privacy-sensitive local data processing:** Tasks involving highly personal data, such as analyzing photos or contacts, can be handled entirely on the device. This ensures that sensitive information never leaves the user's physical hardware.
3. **Offline functionality for core tasks:** AI models can perform essential functions like translation or basic image recognition even when there is no internet connection. This provides reliable performance in areas with poor or no connectivity.
4. **Instantaneous local personalization:** The phone can adapt its interface or suggestions based on immediate, local context, like the current time of day or recent local activity. This level of immediate, context-aware responsiveness is difficult to achieve with remote cloud processing.
5. **Energy efficiency for continuous operation:** Running AI models locally consumes less battery power compared to constantly streaming data to and from a remote server. This allows for longer periods of uninterrupted, power-efficient operation.
