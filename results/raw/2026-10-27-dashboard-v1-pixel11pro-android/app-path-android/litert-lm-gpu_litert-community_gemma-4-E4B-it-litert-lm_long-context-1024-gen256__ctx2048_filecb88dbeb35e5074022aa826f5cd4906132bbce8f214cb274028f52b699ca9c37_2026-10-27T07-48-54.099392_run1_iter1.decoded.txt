Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, zero-latency voice transcription:** On-device models can transcribe speech instantly as it is spoken, without the delay of sending audio to a server and waiting for a response. This is crucial for live captioning during a conversation or a meeting where even a slight delay is disruptive.
2. **Instantaneous gesture recognition for accessibility:** AI running locally can interpret complex hand or body movements in real-time to control the phone interface. This allows users with limited mobility to interact with their device without needing to connect to the internet or wait for cloud processing.
3. **Immediate, private photo tagging and object detection:** A phone can scan a photo and instantly identify objects, people, or scenes without uploading the image to a third-party server. This ensures that sensitive personal photos remain entirely private and are processed locally on the device's hardware.
4. **Offline language translation for basic phrases:** While complex translation requires the cloud, on-device AI can handle quick, essential phrases in a foreign language without connectivity. This is vital for travelers in areas with poor or no internet access.
