1. **Real-time Neural Translation**
   A cloud model might translate text, but it requires a human to pause, review the output, and decide whether to trust the translation or not.
   An on-device AI can instantly translate text in real-time without any human intervention, ensuring the user gets the most accurate translation immediately.

2. **Automatic Video Transcription**
   Cloud models often struggle with audio quality and may hallucinate words when the input is unclear or noisy.
   On-device AI can transcribe video and audio with high accuracy, even in low-light conditions or when the audio is distorted, without needing a human to listen and read.

3. **Instant Data Encryption**
   Cloud models can encrypt data, but they cannot verify the integrity of the encryption keys or the actual data payload.
   On-device AI can perform end-to-end encryption on a device, ensuring that the data is encrypted and unencrypted at the same time, preventing unauthorized access.
