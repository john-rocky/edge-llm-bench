Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot, based on the general capabilities of on-device processing versus cloud-based models:

1. **Real-time, low-latency processing:** On-device AI can perform tasks instantly without needing to send data to the cloud for processing. This is crucial for applications requiring immediate feedback, such as live translation or instant image recognition in a camera feed.
2. **Enhanced privacy and security:** Data processed locally on the device remains on the user's hardware, significantly reducing the risk of sensitive personal information being transmitted or stored on external servers. This makes on-device AI ideal for highly sensitive applications like biometric authentication.
3. **Offline functionality:** On-device models allow the phone to function completely without an internet connection, providing essential utility in areas with poor or no connectivity. This is vital for navigation, basic voice commands, and offline language translation.
