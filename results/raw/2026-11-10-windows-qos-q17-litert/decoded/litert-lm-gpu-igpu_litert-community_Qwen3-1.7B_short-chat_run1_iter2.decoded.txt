On-device AI means using AI technology **on your device**, like your phone, tablet, or computer, instead of sending data to the cloud or a server. It's like having a small AI computer that runs on your device, not in a big server somewhere else. 

For example, if you use a camera on your phone, the AI can help recognize objects, like a cat or a dog, in real time. It doesn't need to send all the data to the internet, which saves bandwidth and makes the AI faster and more efficient. 

On-device AI is becoming more popular because it's better for privacy
