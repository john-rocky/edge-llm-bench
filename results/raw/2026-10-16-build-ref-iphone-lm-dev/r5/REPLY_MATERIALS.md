## D2 — a LiteRT-LM Android build from a git ref, on DDP

1. **What is possible now.** `LITERTLM_REF=<sha | branch | pr/N> android/scripts/build_litert_lm_main.sh` builds that commit and registers it in `android/engine-pins.json` under the label `main-<sha9>` (a sha or the branch main), `<branch>-<sha9>` or `pr<N>-<sha9>`; `./bench matrix <cells> --ddp <id> --engine-build <label> --project <p> --bucket gs://<b>` runs the cells' LiteRT-LM rows with that build, and every record stamps `engineVersion` `<label>`. `android_pins` (the pinned tag, v0.18.0) does not change, and a label's rows never replace the pinned tag's session in a standing cell. Source: `android/README.md` "Build from head or a PR"; `docs/ddp-integration.md` "Route C as `bench matrix --ddp`", bullet `--engine-build`.
2. **With a build made elsewhere (e.g. from an unsubmitted change).** Put its 9 files flat in `<dir>/<label>/` (`litert_lm_main`, `litert_lm_advanced_main` and the 7 GPU `.so` of v0.18.0's set) → `android/scripts/pins_add.py <label> <dir>/<label>` (writes the entry from the files' sha256; `--dry-run` writes nothing; refuses a missing tool, no `.so`, a git-lfs pointer, an existing label) → `BENCH_ANDROID_BIN_DIR=<dir> ./bench matrix <cells> --ddp <id> --engine-build <label> --project <p> --bucket gs://<b>` (add `--dry-run` first: plan only, no network). Source: `android/README.md` "Build from head or a PR".
