Imagine you have a really smart assistant, like a personal helper, but instead of sending all your information to a giant computer in the cloud (like a big server on the internet), **the smarts are actually living right on your device.**

**On-device AI simply means running the Artificial Intelligence directly on your phone, computer, or smart device, instead of needing to send everything to the internet to be processed.**
