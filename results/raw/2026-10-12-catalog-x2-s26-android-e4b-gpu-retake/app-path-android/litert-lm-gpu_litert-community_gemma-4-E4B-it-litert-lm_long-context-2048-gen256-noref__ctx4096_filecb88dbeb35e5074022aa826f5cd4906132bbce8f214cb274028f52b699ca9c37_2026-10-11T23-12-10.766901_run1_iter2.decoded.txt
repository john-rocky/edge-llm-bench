Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, zero-latency voice transcription:** On-device models can transcribe speech instantly as it is spoken, without the delay of sending audio to a server and waiting for a response. This is crucial for live captioning during a conversation or meeting where even a slight delay is disruptive.
2. **Instantaneous gesture recognition for accessibility:** A phone can use its camera or sensors to recognize specific hand gestures in real-time to control the device or interact with apps. Because the processing happens locally, the user does not need an internet connection to perform these actions.
3. **Immediate, private photo tagging and object detection:** AI can scan a photo immediately after it is taken to identify objects, people, or scenes without uploading the image to a third-party server. This ensures that sensitive personal photos remain entirely on the user's device.
4. **Offline language translation for short phrases:** While complex translation requires the cloud, on-device models can handle quick, short phrases or common vocabulary translations without connectivity. This allows travelers to communicate basic needs even in areas with no cellular service.
5. **Local anomaly detection in sensor data
