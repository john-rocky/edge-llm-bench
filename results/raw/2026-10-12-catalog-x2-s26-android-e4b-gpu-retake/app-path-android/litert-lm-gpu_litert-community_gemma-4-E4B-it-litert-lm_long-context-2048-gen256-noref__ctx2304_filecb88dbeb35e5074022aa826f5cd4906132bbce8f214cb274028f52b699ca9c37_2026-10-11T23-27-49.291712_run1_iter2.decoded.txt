Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, zero-latency voice transcription:** On-device models can transcribe speech instantly as it is spoken, without the delay of sending audio to a server and waiting for a response. This is crucial for live captioning during a meeting or conversation where even a slight delay is disruptive.
2. **Instantaneous gesture recognition for accessibility:** A phone can use its camera or sensors to recognize specific hand gestures in real-time to control the device or interact with apps. This allows for immediate control without needing to process the video stream through a remote cloud service.
3. **Local, private image blurring/redaction:** AI can instantly detect and blur sensitive information, like faces or license plates, within a photo before it is even saved or shared. This ensures privacy protection happens at the source, keeping sensitive data entirely off the network.
4. **Offline language translation for basic phrases:** Certain lightweight translation models can run locally to translate short, common phrases while the phone has no internet connection. This provides essential communication capabilities in remote areas where connectivity is unreliable.
