Here are 25 distinct things on-device AI lets a phone do that a cloud model cannot:

1. **Real-time, zero-latency voice transcription:** On-device models can transcribe speech instantly as it is spoken, which is crucial for live captioning or dictation where even a slight delay is disruptive. This immediate processing happens entirely on the phone's processor, ensuring the user experiences no lag while speaking.
2. **Instantaneous local image recognition:** A phone can identify objects, faces, or text within a photo the moment it is taken without needing to upload the image to a server. This allows for immediate actions, such as tagging a photo or translating a sign, without waiting for a network connection.
3. **Privacy-preserving biometric authentication:** Facial recognition or fingerprint scanning performed locally ensures that sensitive biometric data never leaves the device's secure enclave. This prevents the risk of personal identity data being intercepted or stored on a third-party cloud server.
4. **Offline language translation:** On-device translation models allow users to translate text or speech in areas with no internet connectivity. This is vital for travelers or emergency situations where cloud services would be completely unavailable.
