Imagine you have a super-smart assistant, like Siri or Google Assistant.

**Traditional AI** (the old way) is like having that assistant live in a big, powerful computer in the cloud (on the internet). When you ask it a question, your phone has to send your question *over the internet* to that big computer, the computer figures out the answer, and then sends the answer *back* to your phone.

**On-Device AI** is like giving that assistant a tiny, powerful brain that lives *right inside your phone, tablet, or laptop.*
